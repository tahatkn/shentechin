Focus & Attention

Difficulty concentrating has many possible causes, and they look alike from the inside. Sleep debt, chronic stress, an environment full of interruptions and ADHD can all produce the same daily experience. This assessment maps the pattern; it does not name the cause.

Common questions

Is this an ADHD test?

No. ADHD is diagnosed by a qualified clinician through a structured interview, developmental history and often input from people who know you. No online questionnaire can do that — including this one.

Then what is it for?

To give you a clearer description of the pattern. If the difficulties are broad, long-standing and affecting work or relationships, that description is a useful thing to bring to an appointment.

Could it just be sleep?

Very often, yes. Chronic short sleep produces attention problems that closely resemble ADHD. It is worth ruling out first — our sleep assessment is a reasonable starting point.

This is an educational self-assessment, not a medical test. It cannot diagnose anything. Read the medical disclaimer before you start.
