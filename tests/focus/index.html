Focus & Attention

Difficulty concentrating has many possible causes, and they look alike from the inside. Sleep debt, chronic stress, an environment full of interruptions and ADHD can all produce the same daily experience. This assessment maps the pattern; it does not name the cause.

What it looks at

  • Holding attention on a task and finishing what you start
  • How easily you are pulled away, and how long it takes to return
  • Organisation, time management and remembering commitments
  • Restlessness, impulsivity and racing thoughts
  • Mental fatigue and how much of it tracks your sleep

Who it is for

Anyone who reads the same paragraph three times, anyone who starts more than they finish, and anyone wondering whether to raise attention difficulties with a clinician.

Common questions

Is this an ADHD test?

No. ADHD is diagnosed by a qualified clinician through a structured interview, developmental history and often input from people who know you. No online questionnaire can do that — including this one.

Then what is it for?

To give you a clearer description of the pattern. If the difficulties are broad, long-standing and affecting work or relationships, that description is a useful thing to bring to an appointment.

Could it just be sleep?

Very often, yes. Chronic short sleep produces attention problems that closely resemble ADHD. It is worth ruling out first — our sleep assessment is a reasonable starting point.

This is an educational self-assessment, not a medical test. It cannot diagnose anything. Read the medical disclaimer before you start.

Sık sorulanlar

Bu bir DEHB testi mi?

Hayır. DEHB, yetkin bir klinisyen tarafından yapılandırılmış görüşme, gelişim öyküsü ve çoğu zaman sizi tanıyan kişilerden alınan bilgiyle tanılanır. Hiçbir çevrimiçi anket bunu yapamaz — bu da dâhil.

Peki ne işe yarıyor?

Örüntüye dair daha net bir tarif vermeye. Güçlükler geniş, uzun süreli ve işinizi ya da ilişkilerinizi etkiliyorsa, bu tarif bir randevuya götürmeye değer bir bilgidir.

Sadece uykudan olabilir mi?

Çok sık, evet. Kronik kısa uyku, DEHB'ye epeyce benzeyen dikkat sorunları üretir. Önce onu dışlamakta fayda var — uyku değerlendirmemiz makul bir başlangıç noktası.

Bu, eğitim amaçlı bir öz-değerlendirmedir, tıbbi test değildir. Hiçbir şeye teşhis koyamaz. Başlamadan önce tıbbi uyarıyı okuyun.
